### obligee
- 權利人

### Seizure
- Search and seizure: 搜索扣押。
- Warrant of seizure: 扣押令。
- Seizure of property: 扣押財產。 

> A Serzure that is not covered by a search warrant shall be adjudicated by a judeg, except when the item is seized for evidence, or the obligee of the seizure target has given consent.
