## 二、重要例句與考古題解析

### 1. 要約與承諾（Offer & Acceptance）
> **104 年第 57 題**  
> In contract law, if an offeree responds to an offer by making an **counteroffer**, then the original offer is treated as rejected.  
> - 受要約人提出反要約，原要約即告拒絕。

> **109 年第 58 題**  
> **Acceptance** means the manifestation of assent by the offeree to the terms of the offer.  
> - 承諾即受要約人對要約條件表示同意。

> **114 年第 70 題**  
> If the offeree's belief is reasonably induced by the offeror's words or conduct, it is considered a valid offer.  
> - 受要約人基於要約人言行所產生之合理信賴，構成有效要約。

---

### 2. 對價（Consideration）
> **105 年第 59 題**  
> Something of value given by both parties to a contract that induces them to enter into the agreement to exchange mutual performances is called **consideration**.  
> - 對價係指雙方當事人均給付有價值之事物，以激勵彼此締約。

> **107 年第 60 題**  
> A contract requires **consideration** in practical sense—a detriment to the promisee or a benefit to the promisor.  

---

### 3. 契約之相對性（Privity of Contract）
> **105 年第 60 題**  
> Under the doctrine of **privity of contract**, only a party to a contract can be bound by the obligations under the contract, and only a party to a contract can enforce the contractual rights.  
> - 契約僅於當事人間有拘束力。

---

### 4. 不可抗力（Force Majeure）
> **109 年第 59 題**  
> **Force majeure** clauses excuse a party from liability if some unforeseen event beyond the control of that party prevents it from performing its obligations under the contract.  
> - 不可抗力條款：因不可預見之事由，致當事人無法履約時，得免除其責任。

---

### 5. 更新（Novation）
> **113 年第 59 題**  
> A **novation** occurs when a new contract takes the place of either an existing obligation between the same parties or a new party is substituted from an existing obligation.  
> - 以新契約取代舊契約，或以新當事人取代原方當事人。

---

### 6. 約定違約金（Liquidated Damages）
> **113 年第 60 題**  
> **Liquidated damages** are a fixed amount specified in the contract to be paid by the breaching party in case of a breach, provided it is a reasonable forecast of the harm caused by the breach.  
> - 當事人預先約定之違約金額，須為合理預估之損害金額。

---

### 7. 附合契約（Contract of Adhesion）
> **111 年第 59 題**  
> A **contract of adhesion** is a standardized contract drafted by a party with superior bargaining power and offered to the weaker party on a "take it or leave it" basis.  
> - 由優勢地位之一方擬定，他方僅得接受或拒絕，不能磋商修改。

---

### 8. 保密協議（Non-Disclosure Agreement）
> **112 年第 59 題**  
> A **non-disclosure agreement (NDA)** is a contract that requires the parties to keep certain information confidential.  

---

### 9. 欠缺行為能力（Lack of Capacity）
> **112 年第 58 題**  
> A person who lacks mental capacity or is a minor may raise **lack of capacity** as a defense to avoid a contract.  
> - 110 年第 59 題亦考：an **intoxicated** person may lack capacity to form a contract（醉酒者可能欠缺行為能力）。

---

### 10. 授權書（Power of Attorney）
> **114 年第 65 題**  
> A **power of attorney** is a legal document that gives someone the authority to act on behalf of another person in legal or financial matters. A **general** power of attorney provides broad powers while a **special** power of attorney is limited to specific matters.  

---

### 11. 誠信原則（Good Faith）
> **111 年第 60 題**  
> Parties to a contract are expected to deal with each other in **good faith**—honestly and fairly—without intent to defraud or seek unconscionable advantage.  

---

### 12. 契約無效與得撤銷（Void vs. Voidable）
> **106 年第 57 題**  
> A **void** contract is a contract that has no legal force from the beginning. A **voidable** contract is valid and binding unless annulled by a party who was entitled to avoid it.  
> - void = 自始無效；voidable = 有效但得撤銷。

---

## 三、一試重點提示

1. **offer → counteroffer → acceptance** 三步驟：幾乎每年必考，注意 counteroffer 等同拒絕原要約。
2. **consideration**（對價）：英美法獨特概念，與台灣民法有約因（causa）之差異。
3. **privity of contract**（契約相對性）：與民法「債之相對性」對應。
4. **void vs. voidable**：void = 自始確定無效；voidable = 撤銷前仍有效。
5. **force majeure**：不可抗力為法文，但已成英美契約常用條款，與「情事變更」有別。
6. **liquidated damages vs. penalty**：前者為合理預估損害額，後者為懲罰性違約金。
7. **contract of adhesion**（附合契約）：與消保法定型化契約概念吻合。
8. **power of attorney**（授權書）：general（一般）vs. special（特定事項），114 年新考點。
