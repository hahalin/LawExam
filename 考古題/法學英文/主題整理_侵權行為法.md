## 二、重要例句與考古題解析

### 1. 代位責任（Vicarious Liability）
> **107 年第 58 題**  
> Pursuant to Article 188 of R.O.C. Civil Code, an employer shall be jointly and severally liable for any injury which the employee has wrongfully caused to the rights of another in the performance of his duties. In other words, an employer is **vicariously** liable for the negligent acts by his employees.  

- **考點**：僱用人對受僱人職務上之侵權行為負連帶賠償責任（民法第 188 條）。  
- **關鍵字**：vicariously = 代位地、代理地。

> **114 年第 58 題**  
> In certain circumstances, A can be held liable for the torts committed by B simply because B is A's employee or agent or independent contractor. What is the type of the aforementioned tortious liability?  
> 答案：**Vicarious liability**（代位責任）

---

### 2. 無過失責任（Strict Liability）
> **114 年第 59 題**  
> _____ is liability regardless of fault. The plaintiff does not have to prove either that the defendant was at fault or that the defendant was not at fault.  
> 答案：**Strict liability**（無過失責任）

- **考點**：嚴格責任不以過失為要件，原告毋須證明被告有過失。

> **112 年第 56 題**  
> Under Article 191-3 of R.O.C. Civil Code, a person who engages in **ultra-hazardous** activities is liable for damage characteristic to nature of the activity and resulting from it.  
> - 民法第 191-3 條：經營危險事業或活動之人，對所生之損害負賠償責任。

---

### 3. 連帶責任（Joint and Several Liability）
> **114 年第 56 題**  
> Under Article 185 of R.O.C. Civil Code, **joint and several** liability is where the whole or a distinct part of the damage suffered by the victim is attributable to two or more persons.  
> - 民法第 185 條：數人共同不法侵害他人之權利者，連帶負損害賠償責任。

---

### 4. 過失（Negligence）
> **105 年第 57 題**  
> John, driving his truck on the street, was attracted by a burning car on his left side, then hit Mary. John is **not** legally immune—Mary may sue John on **negligence** to recover damages for her personal injury.  

> **113 年第 56 題**（negligence per se 當然過失）  
> An actor is negligent if, without excuse, he/she violates a statute that is designed to protect against the type of accident that the actor's conduct causes. Such rule is usually called **negligence per se**.  
> - 違反保護他人之法律即構成過失。

---

### 5. 注意義務（Duty of Care）
> **110 年第 58 題**  
> Rachel is a babysitter. She left Michael, a two-year-old boy, at home alone. Michael put a fork into an electric plug and was seriously injured. Rachel was negligent for breaching of **duty of care**.  
> - 保母對幼兒負有注意義務，離棄幼兒即違反注意義務。

---

### 6. 因果關係（Causation）
> **107 年第 67 題**  
> Vincent has a 10-minute quarrel with Dan before he goes to work. On Vincent's way to work, a drunk driver hits him. Dan may argue that there is no **causation** between Vincent's injuries and Dan's acts.  
> - 爭吵行為與交通事故間欠缺因果關係。

---

### 7. 公害（Nuisance）
> **114 年第 57 題**  
> Oliver liked to play music extremely loud and it always woke up Mr. and Mrs. Smith at midnight. Oliver was liable to Mr. and Mrs. Smith for **nuisance**.  

> **109 年第 57 題**  
> According to the Restatement (Second) of Torts, a public **nuisance** is an unreasonable interference with a right common to the general public.  

---

### 8. 侵入（Trespass）
> **111 年第 56 題**  
> Eric decided to build a new swimming pool in his backyard. After the construction was done, Ben found the pool was partly built on his land. It is a **trespass** if Eric intentionally built his pool on other's land.  

---

### 9. 誹謗（Defamation）
> **110 年第 56 題**  
> **Defamation** is the term used to describe the tort of making a false statement of fact that injures someone's reputation.  

---

### 10. 非法拘禁（False Imprisonment）
> **108 年第 58 題**  
> Larry locks Kevin, who is an athlete, in a room which has two open windows at a height of 100 cm from the floor. → Kevin 身為運動員可攀窗離開，**不構成** false imprisonment。  
> - 關鍵：被害人是否有合理途徑離開。

---

### 11. 損害賠償類型
> **111 年第 65 題**  
> **Punitive damages** could be awarded in addition to actual damages when the defendant acted with recklessness, malice, or deceit.  

> **111 年第 69 題**  
> **Nominal damages** are a trivial sum of money awarded to a plaintiff whose legal right has been technically violated but who has not established that they are entitled to compensatory damages.  

> **113 年第 58 題**  
> The purpose of tort law is to provide relief for the victim for harm actually done. Damages for these harm are called **compensatory damages**.  

---

### 12. 自甘冒險（Assumption of the Risk）
> **113 年第 57 題**  
> Peter signed a release for personal injury before he took a diving class but he was bitten by a sea snake under water. The strongest argument the school may raise is **assumption of the risk**.  

---

### 13. 集體訴訟（Class Action）
> **113 年第 69 題**  
> A company produces a medication that is widely used. Patients who have taken the medication begin to report severe side effects. The most practical form to pursue such a claim is a **class action**.  

---

## 三、一試重點提示

1. **代位責任 vs. 無過失責任 vs. 連帶責任**：此三者為最高頻考點，幾乎每年必考，務必能區辨。
2. **negligence per se**（當然過失）：違反保護性法規即構成過失，113 年新出題型。
3. **損害賠償三分類**：compensatory / punitive / nominal damages，111 年一次考三種。
4. **侵權行為子類型**：trespass、nuisance、defamation、false imprisonment、battery、assault，需熟悉各自定義與構成要件。
5. **reasonable person standard**（合理人標準）：判斷過失之客觀標準。
6. **assumption of the risk**：被害人事先知悉並自願承擔風險，可作為抗辯。
