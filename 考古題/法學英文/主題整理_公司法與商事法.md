## 二、重要例句與考古題解析

### 1. 股東代位訴訟（Shareholder Derivative Suit）
> **109 年第 62 題**  
> A **shareholder derivative suit** is a lawsuit brought by a shareholder on behalf of the corporation against a third party, usually an officer or director of the corporation.  
> - 股東為公司之利益，對董事或第三人提起之代位訴訟。公司法第 214 條。

---

### 2. 獨立董事（Independent Director）
> **109 年第 61 題**  
> A director is considered **independent** if he/she has no material relationship with the company—that is, one which could, in the view of the board of directors, be reasonably expected to interfere with the exercise of independent judgment.  
> - 獨立董事與公司無重大利害關係，可獨立判斷。

---

### 3. 忠實義務與注意義務
> **111 年第 57 題**  
> A corporate director owes a **duty of loyalty** to the corporation; he must act in the best interest of the corporation, not in his personal interest.  
> - 董事對公司負忠實義務，不得圖謀私利。

> **107 年第 59 題**  
> Under the **business judgment rule**, courts generally will not second-guess the decisions of directors made in good faith and with due care.  
> - 商業判斷法則：法院原則上尊重善意且盡注意義務之董事決策。

---

### 4. 異議股東股份收買請求權（Appraisal Rights）
> **112 年第 61 題**  
> **Appraisal rights** allow dissenting shareholders to demand that the corporation purchase their shares at fair value when they oppose certain fundamental corporate changes such as mergers or amendments to the articles of incorporation.  
> - 反對合併等重大變更之股東，得請求公司以公平價格收買其持股。

---

### 5. 累積投票制（Cumulative Voting）
> **114 年第 68 題**  
> Under **cumulative voting**, each shareholder may cast all of his votes for a single candidate or distribute them among several candidates. This system is designed to protect minority shareholders.  
> - 累積投票制保障少數股東選舉董事之權利。公司法第 198 條。

---

### 6. 控制公司與關係企業
> **114 年第 69 題**  
> A **controlling company** is one that directly or indirectly controls the management of another company. Companies that have a controlling-subordinate relationship are called **affiliate enterprises**.  
> - 控制公司：直接或間接控制他公司之經營。關係企業：具有控制從屬關係之企業。公司法第 369-2 條。

---

### 7. 解任董事（Discharged Director）
> **112 年第 62 題**  
> A director may be **discharged** (removed) by a resolution of the shareholders' meeting. Under certain circumstances, a court may also remove a director.  
> - 董事得由股東會決議解任。

---

### 8. 無表決權特別股
> **113 年第 63 題**  
> A company may issue shares with **no voting rights** or restricted voting rights in certain matters, typically preferred shares (special shares).  
> - 公司得發行無表決權或限制表決權之特別股。公司法第 157 條。

---

### 9. 閉鎖性公司（Close Company）
> **113 年第 62 題**  
> A **close company** (closely held corporation) is one which has a limited number of shareholders (typically 50 or fewer under R.O.C. Company Act) and whose shares are not publicly traded.  
> - 股東人數不超過 50 人，不公開發行之公司。公司法第 356-1 條。

---

### 10. 公司組織型態
> **105 年第 63 題**  
> A **sole proprietorship** is owned by a single individual. A **partnership** involves two or more persons sharing profits and losses. A **corporation** is a separate legal entity with limited liability.  
> - 獨資、合夥、公司三種企業型態。

---

### 11. 揭穿公司面紗（Piercing the Corporate Veil）
> **106 年第 60 題**  
> Under the doctrine of **piercing the corporate veil**, courts may hold shareholders personally liable when the corporate form has been used as a sham or to perpetrate a fraud.  
> - 股東濫用公司型態時，法院得否認公司之獨立人格。

---

## 三、一試重點提示

1. **shareholder derivative suit**（股東代位訴訟）：對應公司法第 214 條，出題率高。
2. **duty of loyalty / duty of care / business judgment rule**：董事義務三大核心概念。
3. **appraisal rights**（異議股東收買請求權）：合併、分割時之保護機制。
4. **cumulative voting**（累積投票制）：少數股東保障，公司法第 198 條。
5. **controlling company / affiliate enterprises**（控制公司／關係企業）：公司法第八章。
6. **piercing the corporate veil**（揭穿公司面紗）：英美法重要概念，考出翻譯。
7. **independent director**（獨立董事）：須與公司無重大利害關係。
8. **close company**（閉鎖性公司）：公司法第 356-1 條以下。
