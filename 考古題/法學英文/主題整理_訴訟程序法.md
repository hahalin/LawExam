## 二、重要例句與考古題解析

### 1. 原告與被告（Plaintiff & Defendant）
> **105 年第 56 題**  
> The **plaintiff** is the party who initiates a lawsuit (also known as an action) before a court.  
> - 原告為向法院提起訴訟之人。

> **106 年概念辨析**  
> On appeal, the party who appeals is the **appellant**, and the party against whom the appeal is filed is the **appellee**.  

---

### 2. 消滅時效（Statute of Limitations）
> **104 年第 59 題**  
> A **statute of limitations** is a law which sets the maximum time after an event within which legal proceedings may be initiated.  
> - 法定期間經過後，權利人不得再提起訴訟。

---

### 3. 禁制令（Injunction）
> **106 年第 65 題**  
> An **injunction** is a court order by which a party is required to do, or to refrain from doing, certain acts.  
> - 法院命令當事人為或不為特定行為。

---

### 4. 指示判決（Directed Verdict）
> **111 年第 70 題**  
> A **directed verdict** is a ruling entered by a trial judge after determining that there is no legally sufficient evidentiary basis for a reasonable jury to reach a different conclusion.  
> - 審判法官認為陪審團在證據上無合理空間做不同判斷時，逕行作出判決。

---

### 5. 既判力（Res Judicata）
> **110 年第 70 題**  
> Under the doctrine of **res judicata**, a final judgment on the merits bars the parties from relitigating issues that were or could have been raised in that action.  
> - 確定判決對當事人具有拘束力，不得就同一事項再行訴訟。

---

### 6. 法庭之友（Amicus Curiae）
> **110 年第 65 題**  
> **Amicus briefs** (amicus curiae) are filed by non-parties who have a strong interest in the subject matter. They provide the court with additional perspectives.  
> - 非訴訟當事人提出之意見書，供法院參考。

---

### 7. 交互詰問（Cross Examination）
> **112 年第 63 題**  
> **Cross examination** is the questioning of a witness by the opposing party after direct examination.  
> - 由對造當事人對證人進行詰問。

---

### 8. 協同意見書與不同意見書
> **111 年第 67 題**  
> A **concurring opinion** is a separate opinion delivered by one or more judges which agrees with the decision of the majority but differs from it in reasoning.  
> - 同意判決結論但理由不同。

> **112 年第 70 題**  
> A **concurring opinion** agrees with the result but offers different reasoning.  

---

### 9. 舉證責任（Burden of Proof）
> **113 年第 70 題**  
> The **burden of proof** generally lies on the party who asserts the claim. In civil cases, the standard is "preponderance of the evidence"; in criminal cases, the standard is "beyond a reasonable doubt."  
> - 民事：優勢證據；刑事：超越合理懷疑。

---

### 10. 集體訴訟（Class Action）
> **113 年第 69 題**  
> A **class action** is a lawsuit in which one or several persons sue on behalf of a larger group of persons who share a common legal interest.  
> - 一人或數人代表具有共同法律利益之多數人提起訴訟。

---

### 11. 法院職權相關
> **108 年第 65 題**  
> A case may be declared **moot** when the controversy is no longer live or when the parties lack a legally cognizable interest in the outcome.  
> - 爭端已消滅或當事人已無法律上利益。

> **108 年第 63 題**  
> When a judge has a personal interest that could affect the outcome, the judge should **recuse** himself from the case.  
> - 法官有利害關係時應自行迴避。

---

### 12. 證據開示（Discovery）
> **104 年第 60 題**  
> In the pre-trial **discovery** phase, parties exchange relevant information and documents. A **deposition** is oral testimony taken under oath before trial.  
> - discovery = 審前證據開示；deposition = 庭外宣誓作證。

---

### 13. 被害人影響陳述（Victim Impact Statements）
> **111 年第 61 題**  
> **Victim impact statements** are written or oral information from crime victims about the impact of the crime upon their lives, presented during sentencing.  
> - 被害人於量刑階段陳述犯罪對其生活之影響。

---

## 三、一試重點提示

1. **plaintiff / defendant / appellant / appellee**：基本訴訟角色，注意上訴後身分轉換。
2. **statute of limitations**（消滅時效）：與民法時效制度對應。
3. **res judicata**（既判力）：拉丁文，需特別記憶，與民訴既判力概念相同。
4. **burden of proof**（舉證責任）：注意民事 vs. 刑事標準不同（preponderance vs. beyond reasonable doubt）。
5. **amicus curiae**（法庭之友）：拉丁文，台灣憲法訴訟法也已引入此制度。
6. **concurring opinion / dissenting opinion**：協同意見、不同意見，大法官解釋常見。
7. **cross examination**（交互詰問）：與刑訴交互詰問制度對應。
8. **class action**（集體訴訟）：與我國團體訴訟制度比較。
