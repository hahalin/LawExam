## 二、重要例句與考古題解析

### 1. 內線交易（Insider Trading）
> **104 年第 66 題**  
> **Insider trading** involves buying or selling a security on the basis of material, non-public information in breach of a fiduciary duty or other relationship of trust and confidence.  
> - 利用重大未公開消息買賣有價證券，違反受託人義務。

> **108 年第 64 題**  
> Under R.O.C. Securities and Exchange Act, a person who learns of a material event affecting the company shall not purchase or sell securities before such information is publicly disclosed. This is the prohibition of **insider trading**.  
> - 證交法第 157-1 條：知悉重大消息者，於消息公開前不得買賣。

---

### 2. 短線交易（Short Swing Transactions）
> **112 年第 67 題**  
> Under Article 157 of the R.O.C. Securities and Exchange Act, **short swing transactions** (or short-swing profits) refer to profits realized by corporate insiders from any purchase and sale, or sale and purchase, of the company's equity securities within a six-month period. The company may claim such profits.  
> - 證交法第 157 條：內部人六個月內反向交易之利益，公司得請求歸入。

---

### 3. 公開收購（Tender Offer）
> **111 年第 62 題**  
> A **tender offer** is a public bid to purchase shares from existing shareholders of a company, usually at a premium above the market price, for a specified period of time.  
> - 以高於市價之價格，向現有股東公開出價收購股份。

---

### 4. 操縱市場（Market Manipulation）
> **106 年第 63 題**  
> **Wash sales** occur when a person simultaneously buys and sells the same security to create the appearance of market activity. **Matched orders** involve two or more persons colluding to trade at prearranged prices.  
> - 沖洗交易：同時買賣同一證券製造交易假象。相對委託：通謀以預定價格交易。證交法第 155 條。

---

### 5. 公開說明書（Prospectus）
> **113 年第 64 題**  
> A **prospectus** is a formal legal document required by securities law to provide details about a public offering to potential investors. It contains information about the company's business, financial conditions, and risks.  
> - 公開說明書：提供投資人有關公司業務、財務及風險之資訊。

---

### 6. 私募與公開發行（Private Placement & Public Offering）
> **108 年第 66 題**  
> A **private placement** is the sale of securities to a relatively small number of select investors without registration. In contrast, a **public offering** (including an **IPO**) requires registration with the competent authority and is offered to the general public.  
> - 私募：向少數特定投資人出售，毋須申報。公開發行：須向主管機關申報並向大眾公開。

---

### 7. 金融監督管理委員會（FSC）
> **110 年第 60 題**  
> Under R.O.C. law, securities issuers who meet certain criteria must **file** with the **Financial Supervisory Commission (FSC)** and comply with ongoing disclosure requirements.  
> - 發行人須向金管會申報並履行持續公開揭露義務。

---

## 三、一試重點提示

1. **insider trading**（內線交易）：最高頻考點，出現在 104、106、108 年，注意「material non-public information」（重大未公開消息）。
2. **short swing transactions**（短線交易歸入權）：證交法第 157 條，六個月內買進賣出或賣出買入。
3. **tender offer**（公開收購）：與經營權爭奪相關。
4. **wash sale / matched orders**（沖洗交易／相對委託）：市場操縱行為的兩種類型。
5. **prospectus**（公開說明書）vs. **registration statement**（申報書）：前者提供投資人，後者向主管機關申報。
6. **private placement vs. public offering**（私募 vs. 公開發行）：是否須向主管機關申報為區別。
7. **FSC filing**（金管會申報）：台灣證券主管機關。
