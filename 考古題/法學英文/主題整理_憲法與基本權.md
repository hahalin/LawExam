## 二、重要例句與考古題解析

### 1. 正當法律程序（Due Process）
> **108 年第 70 題**  
> The Fourteenth Amendment provides that no state shall deprive any person of life, liberty, or property without **due process** of law. **Substantive due process** protects against government interference with fundamental rights. **Procedural due process** requires fair procedures (notice and hearing) before the government deprives a person of life, liberty, or property.  
> - 正當法律程序分為實質與程序兩面。

---

### 2. 平等保護（Equal Protection）
> **109 年第 70 題**  
> Under the **equal protection** clause of the Fourteenth Amendment, the government must treat similarly situated persons in a similar manner. Laws that discriminate based on **sex classifications** are subject to **heightened scrutiny**.  
> - 平等保護條款要求同等對待；性別分類適用中度審查。

> **113 年第 68 題**  
> When a statute creates a **suspect classification** (such as race), it is subject to **strict scrutiny**—the government must show a compelling governmental interest.  

---

### 3. 審查標準（Levels of Scrutiny）
> **107 年第 70 題**  
> U.S. courts apply three levels of scrutiny:  
> - **Strict scrutiny**: narrowly tailored to achieve a compelling governmental interest (race, religion, national origin).  
> - **Intermediate / heightened scrutiny**: substantially related to an important governmental interest (sex, legitimacy).  
> - **Rational basis review**: rationally related to a legitimate governmental interest (economic regulations).

> **114 年第 66 題**  
> The level of **scrutiny** applied to a constitutional challenge depends on the nature of the right involved and the classification used by the law.  

---

### 4. 事前限制（Prior Restraint）
> **110 年第 63 題**  
> **Prior restraint** refers to government actions that prevent speech or expression before it takes place. The U.S. Supreme Court has held that prior restraints on speech are presumptively unconstitutional.  
> - 事前限制推定為違憲。

---

### 5. 明顯而立即的危險（Clear and Present Danger）
> **110 年第 69 題**  
> Under the **clear and present danger** test, the government may restrict speech when the words are used in such circumstances as to create a clear and present danger of bringing about substantive evils that Congress has a right to prevent.  
> - 言論自由之限制基準：須達到「明顯而立即危險」程度。

---

### 6. 編入原則（Incorporation Doctrine）
> **110 年第 62 題**  
> The **incorporation doctrine** is the process through which the U.S. Supreme Court has applied portions of the Bill of Rights to the states through the Due Process Clause of the Fourteenth Amendment.  
> - 透過第十四修正案正當程序條款，將權利法案適用於各州。

---

### 7. 違憲審查（Judicial Review）
> **106 年第 67 題**  
> **Judicial review** is the power of the courts to examine the actions of the legislative and executive branches and to determine whether those actions are consistent with the Constitution.  
> - 法院審查立法與行政行為是否合憲之權力。

---

### 8. 公民投票與罷免（Referendum & Recall）
> **104 年第 68 題**  
> A **referendum** is a direct vote by the electorate on a particular proposal or issue.  

> **112 年第 65 題**  
> A **recall** is a procedure by which voters can remove an elected official from office before the end of the official's term.  
> - 罷免：選民於任期屆滿前免除民選公職人員。

---

### 9. 總統豁免權（Presidential Immunity）
> **111 年第 66 題**  
> **Presidential immunity** is the doctrine that the President of the United States is immune from civil lawsuits for actions taken in his official capacity.  
> - 總統就職務行為享有民事豁免權。

---

### 10. 政黨選區劃分（Partisan Gerrymandering）
> **108 年第 69 題**  
> **Partisan gerrymandering** is the drawing of electoral district boundaries to give one political party an unfair advantage over the other.  
> - 操縱選區界線以圖利特定政黨。

---

### 11. 憲法訴願（Constitutional Complaint）
> **113 年第 67 題**  
> A **constitutional complaint** (Verfassungsbeschwerde) is a mechanism that allows individuals to challenge government actions that violate their constitutional rights before the constitutional court.  
> - 人民就公權力侵害基本權，向憲法法院提起之救濟。與台灣憲法訴訟法之裁判憲法審查類似。

---

### 12. 宣告違憲（Struck Down）
> **111 年第 68 題**  
> When a court declares a statute unconstitutional, the law is said to be **struck down**—it is void and unenforceable.  

---

## 三、一試重點提示

1. **三層審查標準**（strict / intermediate / rational basis）：最高頻憲法考點，幾乎每年必考，務必能區辨適用情形。
2. **due process**（正當法律程序）：分 substantive 與 procedural，常與第十四修正案一起考。
3. **equal protection**（平等保護）：與 suspect classification（可疑分類）、sex classification（性別分類）連動。
4. **prior restraint**（事前限制）：推定違憲，言論自由核心考點。
5. **美國憲法修正案編號**：
   - First → 言論、宗教、集會
   - Fourth → 搜索扣押
   - Fifth → 不自證己罪
   - Sixth → 對質詰問
   - Eighth → 禁止殘忍刑罰
   - Fourteenth → 正當程序、平等保護
6. **incorporation doctrine**（編入原則）：聯邦權利法案適用於各州之機制。
7. **judicial review**（違憲審查）：Marbury v. Madison 確立之原則。
8. **constitutional complaint**（憲法訴願）：德國法概念，與我國憲法訴訟法相呼應。
