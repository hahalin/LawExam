## 二、重要例句與考古題解析

### 1. 重罪與輕罪（Felony & Misdemeanor）
> **105 年第 61 題**  
> Crimes are generally classified as **felonies** (serious crimes punishable by imprisonment for more than one year or by death) and **misdemeanors** (less serious crimes punishable by fine or imprisonment for less than one year).  
> - 重罪：一年以上有期徒刑或死刑；輕罪：一年以下或罰金。

---

### 2. 犯意與犯罪行為（Mens Rea & Actus Reus）
> **108 年第 60 題**  
> In criminal law, for a person to be found guilty of a crime, the prosecution must prove both the **actus reus** (guilty act) and the **mens rea** (guilty mind).  
> - actus reus = 客觀犯罪行為；mens rea = 主觀犯罪意思。

---

### 3. 不自證己罪（Self-Incrimination）
> **110 年第 64 題**  
> The privilege against **self-incrimination** is guaranteed by the Fifth Amendment of the U.S. Constitution. A person may refuse to answer questions or provide testimony that could be used against him in a criminal proceeding.  
> - 美國憲法第五修正案保障不自證己罪特權。

---

### 4. 對質詰問（Confrontation）
> **109 年第 63 題**  
> The Sixth Amendment guarantees the right of **confrontation**—the accused has the right to confront and cross-examine witnesses against him.  
> - 第六修正案：被告有與不利證人對質詰問之權利。

---

### 5. 自白（Confession）
> **110 年第 66 題**  
> A **confession** is a statement made by a person accused of a crime that he/she committed the offense. An involuntary confession obtained through coercion or torture is inadmissible in court.  
> - 非任意性自白（強迫取得）不得作為證據。

---

### 6. 間接證據（Circumstantial Evidence）
> **110 年第 67 題**  
> **Circumstantial evidence** is evidence that relies on an inference to connect it to a conclusion of fact, as opposed to direct evidence which supports the truth of an assertion directly.  
> - 間接證據須透過推論始得認定事實。

---

### 7. 證據排除法則（Exclusionary Rule）
> **110 年第 65 題**  
> The **exclusionary rule** prevents the government from using most evidence gathered in violation of the United States Constitution.  
> - 違法取得之證據不得使用。

---

### 8. 搜索票（Search Warrant）
> **113 年第 65 題**  
> A **search warrant** is a court order that a judge or magistrate issues to authorize law enforcement officers to conduct a search of a person, location, or vehicle for evidence of a crime.  

> **109 年第 64 題**  
> The Fourth Amendment protects people against **unreasonable searches** and seizures by the government.  
> - 第四修正案：禁止不合理搜索與扣押。

---

### 9. 假釋與緩刑（Parole & Probation）
> **104 年第 63 題**  
> **Parole** is the conditional release of a prisoner before the completion of his sentence. **Probation** is a court-imposed criminal sentence that allows the offender to remain in the community under supervision.  
> - parole = 假釋（服刑中釋放）；probation = 緩刑（附條件在社區服刑）。

---

### 10. 赦免（Pardon）
> **104 年第 64 題**  
> A **pardon** is an act of grace by the executive power which exempts an individual from punishment.  
> - 行政權之恩赦行為，免除個人之刑罰。

---

### 11. 量刑比例原則
> **114 年第 67 題**  
> The Eighth Amendment prohibits cruel and unusual punishment. A sentence must be **proportionate** to the crime committed.  
> - 第八修正案：禁止殘忍異常之刑罰，量刑須與犯罪相當。

---

### 12. 各類犯罪類型
> **105 年第 62 題**  
> **Larceny** is the trespassory taking and carrying away of another's personal property with intent to permanently deprive. **Burglary** traditionally involves breaking and entering into a building with the intent to commit a felony. **Embezzlement** is the fraudulent conversion of property by a person who has lawful possession.  

> **112 年第 69 題**  
> **Arson** is the crime of intentionally or recklessly setting fire to a building or other property.  
> - 縱火罪：故意或因重大過失放火。

> **112 年第 64 題**  
> **Smuggling** is the illegal transportation of objects, substances, or people across an international border.  
> - 走私：非法跨越國境運送。

---

### 13. 累犯（Recidivist）
> **104 年第 65 題**  
> A **recidivist** is a person who repeatedly commits criminal offenses. Many jurisdictions impose enhanced penalties on recidivists.  
> - 累犯加重其刑。

---

## 三、一試重點提示

1. **felony vs. misdemeanor**：重罪與輕罪之劃分標準（刑度一年為界）。
2. **mens rea + actus reus**：犯罪成立之雙重要件，拉丁文為常考重點。
3. **Fifth Amendment**（不自證己罪）與 **Sixth Amendment**（對質詰問權）：美國憲法修正案為法學英文必考。
4. **exclusionary rule**（排除法則）：與刑訴證據排除法則對應。
5. **parole vs. probation**：假釋 vs. 緩刑，注意區分。
6. **larceny / burglary / robbery / embezzlement / fraud**：各類財產犯罪英文名稱須辨析。
7. **circumstantial evidence**（間接證據）：與直接證據（direct evidence）相對。
8. **search warrant**（搜索票）：與第四修正案「unreasonable searches and seizures」連動。
