# 法學英文主題分類彙整範例

## 主題：契約（Contract）

### 詞彙與中英對照
- contract：契約
- offer：要約
- acceptance：承諾
- consideration：對價
- breach：違約
- void：無效
- enforceable：可執行的

### 例句與題型
- There is a valid contract, because there is mutual assent of legally capable parties.
  - 有效契約須有雙方意思表示且具備行為能力。
- Adam agreed to sell and Rosa agreed to buy Adam’s house. Unfortunately, Adam did not know that his house has burnt down two days ago. Under Article 246 of R.O.C. Civil Code, the contract is void.
  - 買賣標的物滅失，契約無效。

## 主題：侵權（Tort）

### 詞彙與中英對照
- tort：侵權行為
- negligence：過失
- liability：責任
- damages：損害賠償
- vicarious liability：代位責任
- strict liability：無過失責任

### 例句與題型
- Oliver liked to play music extremely loud and it always woke up Mr. and Mrs. Smith at midnight. Oliver was liable to Mr. and Mrs. Smith for nuisance.
  - 噪音干擾屬於侵權行為中的妨害安寧（nuisance）。
- In certain circumstances, A can be held liable for the torts committed by B simply because B is A’s employee. This is vicarious liability.
  - 僱主對僱員職務上行為負連帶責任。

## 主題：訴訟與程序（Litigation & Procedure）

### 詞彙與中英對照
- plaintiff：原告
- defendant：被告
- appeal：上訴
- judgment：判決
- service of process：送達程序
- statute of limitations：時效

### 例句與題型
- The party who appeals a decision is called appellant.
  - 上訴人稱為 appellant。
- Jonathan may assert statute of limitations as a defense and refuse to pay.
  - 時效抗辯可作為拒絕給付的理由。

---

## 主題：刑法（Criminal Law）

### 詞彙與中英對照
- crime：犯罪
- guilty：有罪
- parole：假釋
- probation：緩刑
- sentencing：量刑

### 例句與題型
- According to Article 77 of the R.O.C. Criminal Code, anyone who is sentenced to a certain number of years’ imprisonment may be considered for parole after he has served half of his sentence.
  - 刑期服滿一半可申請假釋。

---

## 主題：公司法與商事法（Company & Commercial Law）

### 詞彙與中英對照
- shareholder：股東
- director：董事
- supervisor：監察人
- board of directors：董事會
- merger：合併
- appraisal right：股份收買請求權

### 例句與題型
- In a public company limited by shares, the shareholders’ meeting has the power to elect or remove a director.
  - 股東會有權選任或解任董事。
